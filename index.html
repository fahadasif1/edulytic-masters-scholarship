FAQ

Good questions

Anything else? Call us on +92 322 8889848.

Is it free?

Yes. Entry, dinner and the draw are all free, and you will never be asked to pay.

What does the scholarship cover?

The full tuition fees, with no upper limit, for one Master's (postgraduate taught) course at a Edulytic partner university in the UK. It does not cover living costs, accommodation, visa fees, the Immigration Health Surcharge or travel, and the winner must meet the university's normal entry requirements.

Do I need to attend to win?

Yes. Only guests checked in with original photo ID on the night are entered into that evening's draw.

When do seat requests close?

Birmingham: 17:00 on Thursday 12 November. Canterbury: 18:00 on Wednesday 2 December. A city may close earlier if all seats are taken.

What ID do I need?

An original passport or UK photo driving licence. Photocopies and photos on your phone are not accepted, and the name must match your request.

When will I hear back?

Our team contacts you for a few details and reviews your profile. If you are shortlisted, your ticket comes by email before the evening. Check your spam folder if you hear nothing.

Can I bring a friend?

Each seat is for one person. Your friend can send their own request on this page.

Terms

Scholarship draw terms

The key points: free to enter; one 100% tuition fee scholarship per event; register online before requests close for your event (18:00 on the day of your event; Manchester: 17:00 on Wednesday 4 November); attend in person with original photo ID; winner drawn at random on the night.

Read the full terms and conditions
  1. Promoter. Edulytic.
  2. Free entry. The draw is free to enter. No purchase or payment is needed to enter or to win.
  3. Who can enter. UK residents aged 18 or over who hold valid UK immigration permission or settled status. Employees of Edulytic, its group companies and their immediate families cannot enter.
  4. How to enter. (a) Send a seat request through this page before requests close for your chosen event (UK time: 18:00 on the day of the event; Manchester: 17:00 on Wednesday 4 November); (b) receive an approved ticket from Edulytic; (c) attend the event in person and check in with an original passport or UK photo driving licence matching the name on your request. One entry per person, for one event only.
  5. Events and closing dates. Birmingham, Thursday 12 November 2026 (requests close 17:00 on 12 November). Manchester, Thursday 5 November 2026 (requests close 17:00 on 4 November). Canterbury, Wednesday 2 December 2026 (requests close 18:00 on 2 December). Doors open at 14:00 and dinner starts at 15:00.
  6. Seats. Seats at each venue are limited and are given at the Promoter's discretion after reviewing each request. Not every request will receive a ticket.
  7. The prize. One prize per event (three in total): a scholarship covering 100% of the tuition fees, with no upper limit, for one postgraduate taught (Master's) course at a UK partner university of Edulytic. The scholarship is valid only for one-year (full-time) Master's courses at BPP University and cannot be applied to any other university or course length.
  8. What the prize does not cover. Living costs, accommodation, visa application fees, the Immigration Health Surcharge, travel, deposits or any other cost. No cash alternative is offered. The prize cannot be transferred.
  9. Conditions of the prize. The winner must meet the university's academic and English language entry requirements, receive and accept an offer, and meet any UK immigration requirements for the course.
  10. The draw. One winner per event is drawn at random, live at the event, from guests checked in with valid ID. The winner must be present when drawn; if not, another winner is drawn.
  11. Winner announcement. The winner is announced on the night. With the winner's consent, their first name and city may be published on Edulytic's website and social media.
  12. Your data. Personal details are used to manage the event and the draw under the Edulytic privacy policy. Marketing messages are sent only if you opt in.
  13. Social media. This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or WhatsApp. By entering, you release these platforms from any liability connected with the promotion.
  14. Changes. The Promoter may change or cancel an event or the draw if circumstances outside its control require it, and will give notice where possible.
